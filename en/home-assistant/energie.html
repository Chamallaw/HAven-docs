Energy ​

The Énergie (energy) tab shows where electricity comes from and where it goes: right now, and over the whole day.

What you see ​

Card
En ce moment (right now)Flows between sun, grid, battery, house and car, animated by power
Aujourd'hui (today)Production, consumption, self-consumption (share of production used on site), autonomy (share of consumption without the grid), purchases and sales, the day's cost
La journée (the day)The hourly curve, with the forecast solar production for the rest of the day, and tomorrow's
Batterie (battery)Charge, power, the day's curve
CarCharge, range, target charge, estimated finish time; pause or resume charging
Appareils (devices)What the tracked devices use, now and since midnight

Describing it ​

Describe the installation in energy, in propriete.ts, with Home Assistant power sensors (W). Everything is optional; solar production comes from solar.entity.

ts
energy: {
  grid: 'sensor.grid_power',          // positive when buying, negative when selling
  battery: { power: 'sensor.battery_power', level: 'sensor.battery_level', capacity: 10 },
  ev: {
    car: 'car',                       // a car from cars[]: its name and colour
    level: 'sensor.car_battery',
    power: 'sensor.charger_power',
    charging: 'switch.charger',
    range: 'sensor.car_range',
    capacity: 52,
    target: 80,
  },
  devices: [
    { name: 'Water heater', power: 'sensor.water_heater_power' },
    { name: 'Washing machine', power: 'sensor.washing_machine_power' },
  ],
  tariff: { price: 0.2516, offPeak: { price: 0.2068, hours: [['22:00', '06:00']] }, feedIn: 0.04 },
},
Field
gridPower exchanged with the grid: positive when buying, negative when selling
homeHouse consumption; otherwise deduced (solar + grid − battery − car)
battery.powerPositive while charging, negative while powering the house
battery.level, capacityCharge (%) and usable capacity (kWh)
ev.level, power, rangeCar battery (%), charging power, range (km)
ev.chargingThe charger switch: the pause / resume button toggles it
ev.capacity, targetCapacity (kWh) and target charge (%): the finish time follows
devicesTracked devices, via a plug or a power meter
tariffPrice per kWh (€): bought, off-peak (time ranges), sold

Power sign

If the grid or battery flow goes the wrong way, your sensor counts backwards. Create a template sensor in Home Assistant that flips its sign, and give that one to HAven.

Where the numbers come from ​

  • The day is rebuilt from these sensors' history in Home Assistant, re-read every 5 minutes. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
  • The solar forecast comes from Open-Meteo: the forecast irradiance, computed for the pitch and orientation of the slope carrying the panels, and the array's peak power.
  • In demo, a complete installation is simulated: panels, a 10 kWh battery, an electric car and devices.
