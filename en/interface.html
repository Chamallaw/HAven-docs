Tour of the interface ​

The interface fits a phone held in one hand, and spreads out on a tablet or computer. It has three tabs: Maison (home), Tableau (dashboard) and Énergie (energy), at the bottom of the screen on a phone, at the top on a computer.

The top bar ​

On the left:

  • the property name;
  • the scene time: a tap opens the time control;
  • the weather and temperature: a tap opens the details;
  • one initial per person at home (Home Assistant person.* entities).

On the right, on the Maison tab:

  • the tag shows or hides the floating labels above the rooms;
  • the pencil opens the in-page editor;
  • the badge opens the settings. Its colour shows the connection state: Démo (demo), Home Assistant, Connexion… (connecting) or Hors ligne (offline).

The Maison tab ​

The 3D view. One finger rotates the model, two fingers zoom and pan (with a mouse: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom towards the pointer, right-click to pan).

  • Nothing selected: a summary lists the buildings with the number of lit rooms, solar production and the cars (parked or away). Tapping a line selects the building. The Vue d'ensemble (overview) button frames the whole property.
  • A building tapped: the camera moves closer, the other buildings fade, and its panel opens with its rooms, floor by floor: lights, temperatures, heating, shutters, doors, vehicles, solar panels.
  • A room tapped (on the model, in the panel or on its label): everything turns off if one of its lamps is on, everything turns on otherwise.

The cutaway view ​

When the building has floor plans, its panel offers Extérieur / RDC / Étage (outside / ground floor / upper floor). The roof disappears and the chosen floor appears as a furnished dollhouse; neighbouring buildings are cut at the same height. Floors are tinted by each room's temperature (bluish below 20 °C, orange above 21 °C) and a little flame shows heating that is running. Rooms stay in the half-light of an interior; a lit room is bathed in the warm light of its ceiling lamp, brightest under the lamp and soft towards the corners, which also lights its walls and furniture. Tapping a room turns its lights on or off.

The Tableau and Énergie tabs ​

  • Tableau (dashboard): one tile per device, sorted by building then room, with scenes, favourites and search.
  • Énergie (energy): live flows, the day's totals and curve, battery, electric car and the devices using power.

Time and weather ​

The time control plays through the day (play button) or picks a precise time and day: the sun, moon, shadows and season follow. Simulated time is shown in colour in the bar; Maintenant (now) returns to the real time.

The weather comes from Open-Meteo: free, no key, refreshed every 10 minutes. The position sent is rounded to about 1 km. Without internet access, HAven uses Home Assistant's first weather.* entity.

After sunset, the interface darkens by itself so it doesn't dazzle.

Settings ​

The badge at the top right opens the settings:

SettingPurpose
Adresse de Home AssistantSe connecter (connect) to Home Assistant, or Rester en démo (stay in demo) (details)
MétéoLive, or a weather preset in demo mode
PropriétéÉditer (edit) the property, or Relever une position (pick a position) to describe doors and windows
Qualité graphiqueAuto, Haute (high), Moyenne (medium) or Économe (saver)

Graphics quality

Auto measures how long each frame takes and adjusts resolution and effects. Post-processing effects (glow, outline, tilt-shift blur) are only on in high quality. Économe spares the battery of an old phone or a wall tablet.

Install on the home screen ​

HAven is an installable web app:

  • iPhone, iPad: Safari → Share → "Add to Home Screen";
  • Android: Chrome → menu → "Install app";
  • Computer: the install icon in the Chrome or Edge address bar.

It then opens full screen, without a browser bar. For instant offline opening, it must be served over HTTPS: see Hosting.
