propriete.ts ​

Complete reference of a property description. The schema in src/model/property.ts is authoritative: your code editor reads it and completes every field.

Conventions: metres; points [x, z] with x towards the east and z towards the south; positions along a side (at, from, to) in metres from the middle of the section, along the ridge. Fields marked ? are optional. cour is the courtyard side, dos the back side, pignon a gable.

SectionConfig ​

FieldType

roof ?'gable' | 'shed'Two slopes (default) or lean-to
highSide ?1 | -1Lean-to: high side, on the local z axis
eave, ridge ?numberEave and ridge heights (m)
merge ?string[]Neighbouring footprints merged: one volume, one roof
into ?stringRoof extended into this building's (higher) roof, with a valley
against ?stringLean-to against a neighbour: high side at the neighbour's eave
open ?booleanOpen shelter: wooden posts and beam, no walls
skew ?[number, number]Skewed gables (degrees): "start" end, then "end"; positive, the courtyard-side corner moves out. Read from the land registry if absent; [0, 0]: square
